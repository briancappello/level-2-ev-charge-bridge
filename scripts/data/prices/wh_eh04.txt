Description

The ABB EH04-11N is an auxiliary contact block designed for ESB..N and EN..N installation contactors, providing 1 normally open and 1 normally closed contact for signaling and interlocking. Rated insulation voltage 500 V, impulse withstand 4 kV, thermal current 6 A. AC-15 rating 3 A at 415 V; DC-13 rating 0.55 A at 125 V. Minimum switching load >17 V / >5 mA. Tool-free snap-on mounting on the contactor front; IP20 protection; complies with IEC/EN 60947-5-1. 9 mm wide module (one modular spacing).
