Product Description

The Eaton BRN250GF GFCI circuit breaker provides advanced ground fault protection for personnel and equipment, making it ideal for residential and commercial electrical panels. This 50 amp, 2-pole breaker is designed for 120/240 V systems and features a pigtail neutral for easy installation. With a 10 kAIC interrupt rating, it delivers reliable circuit protection against overloads and short circuits. The panel mount design and copper construction ensure durability and secure connections. The ground fault circuit interrupter (GFCI) function helps prevent electrical shock hazards, offering peace of mind for safety compliance. Measuring 6.3 in. long, 3.5 in. wide, and 2.3 in. high, and weighing 1 lb., this breaker fits standard panel configurations. Compatible with a range of applications requiring a 50 amp GFCI breaker, it is a dependable choice for new installations or upgrades. No batteries are required, and the breaker is suitable for use in various environments where ground fault protection is essential.
