What is a Cable Gland?
A cable gland, commonly known as a cable connector or fitting,
is a device designed to attach and secure the end of a cable
to electrical equipment. Cable glands provide strain relief and
typically seal out dust, dirt and even liquids.
                                                                                                                             Pressure Dome
Cable glands are generally used throughout a number of
industries in conjunction with cable and wiring used in electrical
instrumentation and automation systems. They may be used
on all types of electrical power, control, instrumentation, data
and telecommunications cables. A key function of cable glands
is to seal and terminate electrical equipment to ensure that the                                                                                                                                Sealing Sleeve
characteristics of the enclosure which the cable enters can be
maintained.
