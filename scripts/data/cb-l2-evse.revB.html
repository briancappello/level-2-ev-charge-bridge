Build notes

Sizing

40 A is continuous, so the branch breaker and conductors are sized at 125%: a 50 A breaker with 6 AWG NM-B, or 8 AWG THHN in conduit at 75 °C. Every device in the 40 A path (terminals, meter, contactor, cable) is rated at 48 A or more.

Pilot setup

Set the EVSE maximum current to 40 A in EVerest. That gives a 66.7% CP duty cycle. The ChargeBridge module generates the ±12 V pilot and runs the A–F state machine; you only wire CP and its PE reference. If the tethered cable has a fifth wire from the handle latch button, land it on PP (the module supports CCS1 attached-cable button detection); otherwise leave PP open. Keep total CP capacitance under 1600 pF and do not add an external CP clamp diode.

Contactor

Relay driver 1 closes K1 only in state C. The NC aux contact goes to feedback 1 so the module can refuse to start, or fault, if K1 welds. Measure the coil’s pull-in current against the driver rating in the datasheet before power-up.

Ground-fault and ground monitoring

The eval board has no residual current measurement or ground-continuity monitor of its own. CB1 covers personnel protection; RCM1 adds in-unit detection. A UL 2231 CCID20 with auto-reclose and ground monitoring is not part of this BOM.

Before energising. This is a draft for a one-off build, not a listed product. The ChargeBridge module is certified to its own standards, but the assembled charger is not UL 2594 listed. Pull a permit, have the branch circuit inspected, and check with your insurer. Bench-test the pilot and contactor at 24 V with a J1772 EV simulator before putting 240 V on it.
